1. Introduction and Our Commitment

Welcome to SEOcom ("we," "our," "us"). Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website www.seocom.com (the "Site").

We are committed to being transparent about our data practices. This policy is designed to help you understand:

  • What information we collect from you
  • How we use that information
  • Who we share it with
  • How we protect it
  • What rights you have regarding your data

By using our Site, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use our Site.

SEOcom is a leading digital marketing agency dedicated to helping businesses grow online. We value your trust and are dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of data privacy and security.

2. Information We Collect

We collect different types of information to provide and improve our services. Here is a detailed breakdown:

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

When you interact with our Site, we may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide:

  • Full Name — To personalize your experience and address you properly
  • Email Address — To send newsletters, updates, and marketing communications
  • Phone Number — For consultation scheduling and customer support
  • Company Name — To understand your business needs
  • Project Details — Information about your digital marketing requirements
  • Account Credentials — Username and password for account access

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Site, we automatically collect certain technical information:

  • IP Address — For analytics, security, and fraud prevention
  • Browser Type and Version — To optimize site performance
  • Device Information — Desktop, mobile, or tablet
  • Pages Visited — To understand user behavior and improve content
  • Time and Date of Visit — For analytics purposes
  • Referring URL — To understand how you found us
  • Click Activity — To track user engagement

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Service Delivery — To provide and improve our digital marketing services
  • Customer Support — To respond to your inquiries and resolve issues
  • Personalization — To tailor our services to your business needs
  • Communication — To send marketing updates, newsletters, and offers (with consent)
  • Site Improvement — To analyze usage patterns and enhance user experience
  • Security — To protect against fraud, unauthorized access, and abuse
  • Legal Compliance — To comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Marketing — To send relevant content and offers (only with your permission)

We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes without your explicit consent. Your trust is our priority.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience. Here's what you need to know:

4.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences and understand how you interact with our Site.

4.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies — Required for core functionality like navigation and secure login
  • Performance Cookies — Help us understand how visitors use our Site (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Functional Cookies — Remember your preferences like language and currency settings
  • Targeting/Advertising Cookies — Deliver relevant ads based on your interests (with consent)

4.3 Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View the cookies stored on your device
  • Delete cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Disable all cookies

Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain features and functionality of our Site.

We work with trusted third-party service providers to operate our business. These include:

  • Google Analytics — For website analytics and performance tracking
  • Payment Processors — For secure and encrypted payment handling
  • Email Marketing Services — For newsletters and promotional emails
  • Cloud Hosting Providers — For storing and managing data securely
  • Project Management Tools — For managing client projects and communications

These third parties have their own privacy policies and may collect information about you. We recommend reviewing their policies. We are not responsible for their data practices.

6. How We Protect Your Data

We take data security seriously and implement multiple layers of protection:

  • SSL Encryption — All data transmitted between your browser and our server is encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols
  • Firewalls — We use advanced firewalls to prevent unauthorized access
  • Access Controls — Only authorized personnel have access to personal information
  • Regular Security Audits — We conduct periodic reviews of our security systems
  • Data Minimization — We only collect information that is necessary for our services
  • Secure Payment Processing — Payment information is processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways

While we implement robust security measures, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to protecting your data to the best of our ability.

We follow industry best practices to ensure your personal information remains safe and secure at all times.

7. Your Legal Rights

Under data protection laws, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access — You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Correction — You can update or correct your personal information
  • Right to Deletion — You can request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Restriction — You can restrict how we process your data
  • Right to Data Portability — You can request your data in a portable format
  • Right to Object — You can object to certain types of data processing
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — You can withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint — You can file a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@seocom.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Site is designed for a general audience and is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. We will take steps to remove such information from our systems.

We encourage parents and guardians to monitor their children's online activity and educate them about safe internet practices.

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Sending an email notification (if we have your email address)
  • Posting a prominent notice on our Site

We encourage you to review this policy regularly to stay informed about how we protect your information.
